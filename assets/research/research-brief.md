# Research brief

Adapt the fields that matter to the task. Keep assumptions distinct from facts and avoid collecting unnecessary background merely to fill a form.

## Decision and evidence gap

- Decision owner, feasible actions and decision deadline:
- Observed symptom or opportunity, with the evidence currently available:
- Plausible alternative explanations:
- Information still needed, why it could change the decision, and whether existing records can answer it:
- Course/client constraints and requested deliverable:

## Research questions

| ID | Answerable research question | Population/context/time | Evidence or comparison needed | Decision use | Primary or exploratory |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use neutral questions rather than predicted conclusions. Add a hypothesis and planned contrast only when a confirmatory question calls for them.

## Proposed design

- Exploratory, descriptive, experimental, longitudinal or mixed approach, with rationale:
- Target population, reachable frame, eligibility, recruitment/selection method and coverage limits:
- Collection mode, independent unit and any repeated respondent/task/site structure:
- Constructs/outcomes, candidate measures and known measurement limitations:
- Assignment/control/exposure plan when studying an intervention:
- Sample/precision or power assumptions, relevant subgroup needs and resource constraints:
